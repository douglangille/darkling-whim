*What I'm actively working on as of November 2026.*

## Current Writing

**Weekly Flash Fiction Commitment**
- Holding myself to the commitment to write flash fiction nearly every week. It's my focus for getting my reps in
- Mix of new drafts and revised older works getting fresh attention
- Using this as consistent creative practice and skill-building

**Novels in Progress**
- Noir/ghost story set in Halifax. Still my top priority to get out the door, but I have to go back and rewrite its shitty first draft from scratch. I'm not happy with how it came out. I just haven't summoned up the energy, but winter is coming and I will definitely do that.
- Vampires VS Faeries in SPAAACE! On the back burner.
- Zombie-fightin' character study. In a similar state, but a lot rougher.

I'm not under any particular deadline for any of this. I'm just happy to be writing and creating.

**Also growing right now**: one of the interesting things that comes out of writing flash fiction is that I'm inadvertently growing out my shared universes for other stories. Eventually one of them might end up being a novel all on its own.

## Active Reading

Two archives for craft study right now:

- **Jack Whyte's *Dream of Eagles* series** – Rereading the complete cycle from the very start, for scope and multi-book character development. I'm partway through *The Skystone* and enjoying the reread ([my 2013 reader's guide](https://digital.douglangille.ca/essential-jack-whyte/))
- **Robert Service's collected poetry** – Studying rhythm and narrative power in verse (from my grandfather's copy). I'm always looking at it

## Tech Side Projects

Documenting AI integration experiments for creative workflows at [Digital Doug](https://digital.douglangille.ca). Exploring tools and techniques while maintaining the human spark in the creative process.
